Teacher schedule

Set up the class day

Add each period, pick when it starts and ends, and choose how long the entry, warm-up and clean-up sounds should run. The clock does the rest — no one has to press play between periods.

Ready for the board?

Tap start once at the beginning of the day to unlock sound in this browser tab. After that, the clock runs itself — open the board and it will follow the schedule below.

Changes how the display board looks when it's on the projector — playful and bouncy for younger grades, minimal and mature for older ones. Timing works exactly the same either way.
Lower school: this character walks a little maze to their seat during entry. Upper school: it wanders around behind the countdown. Purely for fun — swap it any time, or turn it off.
Most schools run the same schedule every day — but if Wednesdays have specials, an assembly day, or any other different lineup, add a separate schedule for just those days. Each weekday belongs to exactly one schedule.
Sound library

Royalty-free sounds, generated in the browser

These tracks are synthesized on the spot — nothing downloaded, nothing to license — so they're safe to loop all day. Preview them here, then assign them to a period's entry, warm-up or clean-up slot. You can also upload your own royalty-free file for any slot.

Built-in tracks

Grouped by vibe — Entry usually wants Upbeat, Warm-up wants Instrumental or White noise, Clean-up wants a Chime.

Your uploads

Upload a royalty-free MP3 or WAV you have the rights to use. Files stay in this browser only — they're not sent anywhere.
